### What this shows
>> price_usage prices one concrete usage record at list rates.
>> estimate_run_cost prices the same workload on every model.
>> compare_models_for_task re-ranks by cost per SUCCESSFUL task —
>>   the cheapest token price is not the cheapest solved task.
>> record_spend + budget_status keep a per-task ledger: spend is
>>   visible during the run, not on next month's bill.
